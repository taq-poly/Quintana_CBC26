Toxocara canis: what else is in the reads

The genome readset (2014 adult worms) and the 6 adult RNA-seq samples (Kansas 2023) are fully screened. Every detection below was confirmed by mapping, not just by a classifier hit.

Why the bleed-through calls are firm: M_T10's 1,149 TcLA segment-1 reads cover 96% of the segment, which would look like an infection on breadth alone. But at diagnostic sites they carry M_T6's alleles (60 bases) plus M_T4's (9). A worm's own infection wouldn't be a mixture of two other worms' viruses. The bleed-through rate works out to about 0.07–0.2% of the source sample's reads.

Database error: TcLA segment proteins match T. canis "antigens" ANT-5, ANT-30 and ANT-34 in UniProt at 98.6–99.7%. Those larval "antigens" appear to be viral proteins.

Scope and limitations

  • RNA-seq: the 6 adult worms of PRJNA1041894 were screened; the 9 larval samples in the same BioProject were not.
  • The optional spike-in detection-limit test was not run.
